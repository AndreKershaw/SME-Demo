HOUSE STYLE — NUMBERS (agent reference)

GENERAL
→ Spell out numbers under 10 and numbers that begin a sentence.
→ Use numerals for 10+.
→ Same rule for ordinals (first place; 12 start next week).
→ Always numerals for: addresses, ages, court votes, dates/years/decades, decimals/%/fractions >1, dimensions, distances, 1 million+, speeds, temperatures.
→ Centuries: ninth century; 20th century.
→ School grades: first–ninth spelled; 10+ numerals.
→ Commas in numbers 1,000+.

KEYWORDS EXCEPTION
→ Numerals under 10 OK in keyword-led subheads; spell out in body.

DATES
→ Figures for dates/years. Spell out day and month. No st/nd/rd/th.
→ Yes: Saturday, January 24, 2012.

FRACTIONS / DECIMALS
→ Spell out fractions except dimensions (two-thirds; 8 3/4 inches).
→ Decimals as numerals; leading 0 for amounts <1 (0.25).

LISTS
→ Parallel structure. Introduce with a short phrase/sentence.
→ Capitalize first word after each bullet.
→ Bolded bullet ledes use sentence case, not title case.
→ Prefer subheads over bullets if content runs long (~88+ words / multi-paragraph).
→ No bullets inside subheads. Avoid nested bullets.

PERCENTAGES
→ Use % with no space; always numerals (3.25%, 0.25%). Never “percent.”

RANGES
→ Hyphen, no spaces: 20-30 days.
→ Repeat unit/%/$ on both sides when needed: 10%-12%; $11 million-$21 million.

MONEY
→ Always numerals with symbol: $2, $19.99.

PHONE
→ Dashes, no spaces: 555-867-5309; +1-404-123-4567.

TEMPERATURE
→ Numerals except zero. Use ° symbol, not the word “degree.”
→ Spell out Fahrenheit and minus: 98° Fahrenheit; minus 10° Fahrenheit.

TIME
→ Numerals + spaced a.m./p.m.: 7 a.m.; 7:30 p.m. (not 7 AM).
→ No :00 for on-the-hour.
→ Ranges with hyphen: 7 a.m.-10:30 p.m.
→ Default time zone EST when scheduling matters.
→ Recent decades: the ’90s; older: the 1890s.
