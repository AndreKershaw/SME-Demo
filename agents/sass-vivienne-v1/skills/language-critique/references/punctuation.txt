HOUSE STYLE — PUNCTUATION (agent reference)

AMPERSANDS
→ Do not use & unless part of a brand name (Ben & Jerry’s). Prefer “and.”

APOSTROPHES / POSSESSIVES
→ Singular: add ’s (Sam’s).
→ Singular ending in s: Chris’ doughnut; business’s best recipe.
→ Plural ending in s: managers’ doughnuts.

BULLET PUNCTUATION
→ Period after full-sentence bullets.
→ Period after bullets that complete an introductory stem into a full sentence.
→ No period after fragments that do not complete a stem (and single-word stem completions: skip periods).
→ Colon or period may separate bolded lede from rest — be consistent.
→ Bold the colon with the bolded lede text.

COLONS
→ Use to introduce a bulleted list or set off a bullet lede.
→ Do not use a colon to offset a separate thought — use an em dash instead.
→ No: He could only think about one thing all day: the cookies…
→ Yes: He could only think about one thing all day — the cookies…

COMMAS
→ No Oxford/serial comma.
→ Yes: singing off-key, eating the last Oreo and finger painting.
→ Comma before “but” only when joining two independent clauses.
→ Short introductory words (But, Finally) usually need no comma.

HYPHENS AND DASHES
→ Hyphen, no spaces: first-time user; Monday-Friday; ranges.
→ Em dash with spaces on both sides for asides/shifts: Walmart — the retail giant — is…
→ Do not use en dashes.
